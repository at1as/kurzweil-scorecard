## Remaining gaps and caveats

- Original book editions remain uncollated. Complete extraction of the 147-item 2009 corpus, unextracted scenarios and remaining interviews is still unfinished. This release does not establish lifetime coverage.
- The original v0.3 package has 15 blank outcome-reference locators. All 15 are now resolved in the separate 2026-10-18 citation supplement; the raw imported fields remain unchanged. Source-specific findings and support limits are displayed in the live evidence entries.
- 48 alternative-reading entries omit separate deadline/review rationale fields but include a combined rationale and both outcomes. Both outcomes and the supplied combined explanation are displayed.
- Timing has 2 exact-event records, 33 documented-by bounds, 2 approximate editorial era bounds and 47 unestablished first dates. Unestablished first dates do not erase completed outcomes; exact timing analysis remains sparse.
- K036 is a material deadline-policy choice: reference 2025 versus alternative 2026. Native denominators differ. Comparison uses the equal cohort rather than mixing those rates.
- Broader readings change conditions in many cases, including mechanism and adoption thresholds. They are exposed beside the broader verdict and do not replace the reference criterion.
- Three scored stronger-than-source comparisons (K041, K064, K083) and one warning-only comparator (K048) are excluded from all aggregate rates. The extra K048 object is harmless schema variation beyond the handoff's three scored comparator examples.
- 46 legacy dispositions explicitly assess conditional imported wording; they are useful historical outcomes but are not confirmed extra Kurzweil forecasts. They never add to author denominators.

The integration checks package consistency, not independent historical fact-checking of every editorial judgment or live availability of all 98 URLs.
